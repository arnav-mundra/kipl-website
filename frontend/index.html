What we do

Six domains, one accountable partner

  1. 01

    Operation & Maintenance

    The day-to-day activities that keep every system and piece of equipment performing reliably — integrating managerial, financial, and technical dimensions so your facility's ecosystem works in harmony. Covers operational assistance, start-up activities, productivity improvement, asset optimization, and process control.

  3. 03

    Material Handling

    Thoughtfully designed facility layouts and cost-effective flow management across raw material, work-in-progress, finished goods, and scrap disposal — supported by our in-house fleet of hydras, cranes, and trailers.

    Mining & Logistics

    A dependable, disciplined operation for high-volume mineral transportation — coal, iron ore, and other bulk commodities — plus internal shifting between stockyards, conveyor-feeding points, and storage bunkers with real-time monitoring.

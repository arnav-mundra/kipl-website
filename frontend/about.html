Who we are

Built on people, processes & performance since 2009.

KIPL is the flagship company of Kainsara Group, offering engineering support solutions to Odisha's aluminum, steel, coal, and power sectors — from mechanical maintenance to workforce and asset management.

Our story

Kainsara Infraprojects Pvt Ltd (KIPL) was founded in 2009 by Somnath Naik with a vision to contribute to Odisha's industrial growth. Today KIPL is the flagship company of Kainsara Group, a multi-discipline engineering services company serving the aluminum, steel, coal, and power sectors.

Our work spans mechanical maintenance, operational assistance, workforce management, and operating asset management — backed by our own fabrication workshop in Jharsuguda.

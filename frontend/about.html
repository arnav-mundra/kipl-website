Who we are

Built on people, processes & performance since 2009.

KIPL is the flagship company of Kainsara Group, offering engineering support solutions to Odisha's aluminum, steel, coal, and power sectors — from mechanical maintenance to workforce and asset management.

Our story

It barely matters what you are; what truly matters is how the world perceives you. And that perception is shaped by you — by the values you project and the excellence you deliver.

Kainsara Infraprojects Pvt Ltd (KIPL) is the flagship company of Kainsara Group, a multi-discipline engineering services company. Founded in 2009 by Somnath Naik, we deliver high-quality engineering support solutions to Odisha's aluminum, steel, coal, and power sectors — backed by our own fabrication workshop in Jharsuguda.

  • Mechanical Maintenance
  • Operational Assistance
  • Workforce Management
  • Operating Asset Management
  • Global Technology Solutions

From the top

MD's Desk

Somnath Naik

Managing Director

Workmen are an integral part of this company. They work day in and day out to make sure that their company remains in good shape and they themselves sustain and grow along with the company. Hence, it becomes my duty to appreciate these workmen and encourage them. Nothing is more important than a human life, and I, as the Managing Director of the company, ensure it by implementation of best HSE practices. At the same time, we ensure our workmen give 100% of their efforts at the workplace.

We are committed to our responsibility towards society through Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR). "Healthy Workforce builds Healthy Work Environment in Company" — we implement the best practice in the areas of health & safety for all our employees.

The employees, being the most valuable assets of the Company, are continuously trained to keep pace with the fast-changing technological advancements. We would like to establish ourselves in the market as a Quality Service Organisation, for which each one of you — who is associated or gets associated with us — plays a vital role.

— Somnath Naik, Managing Director
